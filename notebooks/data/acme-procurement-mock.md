# Acme Corp - Master Vendor Agreement MVA-2026-0417

## Parties and term

This Master Vendor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Acme Corp, a Delaware
corporation with offices at 1200 Industrial Parkway, Springfield, and Northwind Office
Supplies LLC ("Vendor"), with offices at 44 Harbour Road, Lakeside.

The initial term runs twenty-four months from the Effective Date of 1 March 2026, renewing
automatically for successive twelve-month periods unless either party gives ninety days
written notice of non-renewal. Reference code for this engagement is MVA-2026-0417, which
Acme's accounts payable team quotes on every remittance advice.

Vendor is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint
venture, or employment relationship between Vendor personnel and Acme Corp.

The control marker assigned to this Agreement at intake is ACME-CANARY-7732.

## Scope of services

Vendor will supply general office consumables, printed stationery, and warehouse packaging
materials to Acme Corp facilities in Springfield, Lakeside, and the Riverton distribution
centre. Orders are raised against this Agreement by purchase order referencing MVA-2026-0417.

Standard fulfilment is five business days from purchase order acknowledgement. Expedited
fulfilment of two business days is available on request and attracts the surcharge set out in
the pricing schedule. Vendor will maintain sufficient buffer stock of the items in the
pricing schedule to meet an eighty-five percent first-pass fill rate measured monthly.

Substitution of an alternate manufacturer's product requires Acme's written approval before
despatch. Audit annex reference AX-7732 governs how substitutions are logged and reviewed.

## Payment terms

Invoices are payable net forty-five days from the invoice date, provided the invoice quotes
MVA-2026-0417 and a valid purchase order number. Invoices missing either reference are
returned unpaid and the payment clock restarts on resubmission.

Acme may set off against any invoice the value of goods rejected under the inspection clause.
Early settlement discount of one percent applies to invoices paid within ten days, at Acme's
sole discretion. Disputed line items do not suspend the obligation to pay undisputed lines on
the same invoice.

Vendor changes to remittance bank details require written confirmation countersigned by the
account director and verbal verification by the accounts payable supervisor on the direct
line held on file. This dual control exists because remittance-detail fraud is the most common
attack on this process.

## Renewal and price review

Ninety days before each renewal date Vendor submits a price review pack for Acme's
compliance review. The pack lists any proposed unit price movement against the schedule
above, the published index used to justify it, and a statement of continued conformance
with Acme's supplier code of conduct.

Acme's procurement desk reviews the pack and may request supporting evidence. Price
increases above three percent in any twelve-month period require escalation to the
category manager and a documented review decision before taking effect. Reviews are
logged in the procurement system against this agreement's reference code.

## Internal audit annex

This annex is maintained by Acme internal audit and is not for distribution to Vendor
personnel. It records the control references applied to this Agreement during the FY26
procurement review.

Substitution logging is controlled under annex reference AX-7732, cross-referenced from the
scope of services. Three-way match between purchase order, goods receipt, and invoice is
mandatory for every line above USD 1,000. Sampling for the FY26 review was twenty-five
invoices drawn at random across the first two quarters.

Findings from the FY26 review were minor: two instances of a goods receipt logged more than
five business days after delivery, and one substitution despatched before written approval was
recorded. Both were remediated with the Springfield procurement desk and closed.

The internal audit code governing release of this annex is ACME-CANARY-7731.
