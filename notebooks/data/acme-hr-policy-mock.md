# Acme Corp - Employee Handbook HB-2026-R3

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue twenty-five days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accruing at 2.08 days per completed month of service. Part-time employees accrue pro
rata against contracted hours.

Up to five unused days may be carried into the following year and must be taken before
31 March or they lapse. Carry-over above five days requires written approval from the
category manager and is granted only where leave was refused for operational reasons.

Requests are raised in the leave portal at least ten working days before the first day
of absence. Requests of more than ten consecutive working days need line manager
approval and are assessed against team coverage. Acme does not pay in lieu of untaken
annual leave except on termination, where the balance is settled in the final pay run.

Public holidays are additional to the twenty-five day entitlement. Employees at the
Riverton distribution centre work a shift pattern and receive a day in lieu for any
public holiday worked.

## Parental and family leave

Birth parents are entitled to twenty-six weeks of parental leave, of which the first
sixteen weeks are paid at one hundred percent of base salary and the remaining ten weeks
at sixty percent. Entitlement begins on the first day of employment; there is no
qualifying service period.

Secondary parents receive eight weeks at one hundred percent of base salary, which may
be taken in up to three separate blocks within the first fifty-two weeks after birth or
placement. Adoption and surrogacy are treated identically to birth.

Notification of intended leave dates is required fifteen weeks before the expected week
of birth where practicable. Dates may be changed once without penalty on four weeks
notice.

Carer's leave of five paid days per year is available for a dependant's illness and does
not reduce annual leave. Bereavement leave is five paid days for an immediate family
member and two days otherwise. Neither requires a medical certificate.

## Sick leave and medical certification

Employees receive ten days of paid sick leave per calendar year, which does not carry
over. Absence must be reported to the line manager before the start of the scheduled
shift, or as soon as practicable.

A self-certification form covers absences of one to three consecutive working days. A
medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required from the fourth
consecutive working day. Certificates are submitted to HR, never to the line manager,
and are held in the confidential medical file rather than the personnel record.

Absence beyond ten days in a rolling twelve months moves to statutory sick pay and
triggers an occupational health referral. The referral is supportive, not disciplinary,
and its report is released to the manager as a fitness-for-work summary only, without
clinical detail.

## Benefits and enrolment

The annual enrolment window runs from 1 November to 30 November for coverage starting
1 January. Outside the window, changes require a qualifying life event - marriage,
birth or adoption, a partner's loss of coverage, or relocation - notified within
thirty days.

Medical cover is offered at three tiers. Acme contributes eighty percent of the
employee-only premium at every tier and fifty percent of dependant premiums. Dental and
vision are bundled with tiers two and three.

The retirement plan matches employee contributions to six percent of base salary, vesting
over three years at one third per completed year. Life assurance is provided at four
times base salary at no cost to the employee.

An education allowance of USD 3,000 per calendar year covers role-relevant study, claimed
through the expense process with evidence of completion.

## Payroll calendar and pay queries

Salaried employees are paid monthly on the last working day of the month, covering the
calendar month. Shift employees at Riverton are paid fortnightly on alternate Fridays,
covering the fortnight ending the preceding Sunday.

Timesheets for shift employees close at 23:59 on the Sunday. A timesheet submitted after
close moves to the following pay run; the hours are not lost.

Pay queries go to the payroll desk through the HR portal. Payroll aims to respond within
three working days. An underpayment confirmed as an Acme error is corrected by off-cycle
payment within five working days rather than waiting for the next run.

Overpayments are recovered by agreed instalments over a period no shorter than the period
over which the overpayment occurred. HR cannot discuss another employee's pay, pay band,
or payroll record with anyone other than that employee.

## Expense claims

Claims are submitted within sixty days of the expense being incurred, with an itemised
receipt for anything above USD 25. Claims older than ninety days are declined unless the
delay was caused by Acme.

Approved categories are travel booked through the corporate tool, accommodation to the
site cap, subsistence at USD 55 per full day away, and role-relevant professional
subscriptions. Alcohol, fines, personal travel segments and in-room entertainment are not
reimbursable.

Mileage in a personal vehicle is reimbursed at 0.58 per mile, inclusive of fuel and wear.
A manager approves claims up to USD 2,500; above that the category manager also approves.
Reimbursement is paid with the next scheduled pay run.

## Conduct, grievance and escalation

Employees raise concerns first with their line manager. Where that is not appropriate, or
the concern is about the manager, it goes directly to an HR business partner. Both routes
are legitimate and choosing the second is never held against the employee.

A formal grievance is acknowledged within five working days and heard within fifteen. The
employee may be accompanied by a colleague or a representative. The outcome is given in
writing with the right of appeal within ten working days to a manager not previously
involved.

Disciplinary matters follow the same staged pattern - informal discussion, written
warning, final written warning, dismissal - with investigation before any stage above
informal. Gross misconduct may result in dismissal without prior warnings.

Retaliation against anyone raising a concern in good faith is itself gross misconduct.
